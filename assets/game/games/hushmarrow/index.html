Hushmarrow

The house has been empty for years. Something in it still listens.

Every place has four areas and a task in each. Finish them all, then reach the exit. Your tasks are listed in the top right. Clear a level to unlock the next one. You can replay any cleared level below. Your light is dying. Whatever lives here hears every footstep, and louder still when you run.

WASD to move, mouse to look, hold Shift to run, F to switch your flashlight on or off, click or Space to fire a pistol if you find one (a hit stuns the monster for 30 seconds), and press E at a closet to hide inside. It cannot see you there, but it may wait outside. On a phone, drag the left side to move and the right side to look, and tap the Hide button near a closet. Headphones recommended.
